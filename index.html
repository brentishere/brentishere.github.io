Live radar and severe weather alerts, around the clock

NorthBamaWX watches the radar for North Alabama and Southern Tennessee every minute of every day. When the National Weather Service issues a warning for our counties, the stream switches to the storm, maps the warning, and reads it out loud right away, day or night.

    What the stream does

    Everything on the stream runs on its own. No one has to be awake for the warning to reach you.

    Spoken warnings

    Tornado, severe thunderstorm and flash flood warnings are read aloud the moment they're issued, with the warning outlined on the map.

    Storm mode

    During a warning the stream locks onto the local radar and stays there until the threat is over.

    Hourly updates

    Current conditions, the forecast and the outlook from the Storm Prediction Center, spoken at the top of every hour.

    Tropics

    Hurricane and tropical storm tracking with observed winds, storm surge and damage reports when a storm threatens the Gulf Coast.
