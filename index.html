Step 2: Install the Browser Extension

AegisCrawler comes with a friendly PageResearch Agent extension for Chrome. After downloading, you'll find the extension folder. Simply open your Chrome browser, go to the extensions page (menu → More tools → Extensions), turn on "Developer mode", click "Load unpacked", and select the extension folder.

Step 3: Record Your First Workflow

Now comes the fun part! Open the extension on any website, click the "Record" button, and start doing what you normally would. Click buttons, type text, scroll — AegisCrawler is watching. When you're done, hit "Stop" and give your workflow a name (like "Morning price check").

Step 4: Schedule & Relax

Your recorded workflow is now saved. You can run it immediately with the "Play" button, or set it on a schedule for automatic execution. Want a daily 8am check? Just set it and forget it. AegisCrawler handles the rest, saving all collected data in a neat, organized database.

🖥️ What's Under the Hood (in plain language)

  • PageResearch Agent Extension — Your control panel. This tool records your actions and manages workflows, all through a simple interface right in your browser.
  • Go Server (the Brain) — This is the scheduler that decides when your workflows should run. It's fast, reliable, and handles multiple tasks at once without breaking a sweat.
  • ScriptCat Workers (the Hands) — These are the real browsers that carry out your recorded actions. They carefully follow the rules you created, interacting with websites just like you do.
  • DSL Rules (the Memory) — A clean, human-readable set of instructions that describes your actions. It's easy to scan and understand — no programmer required.
  • SQLite (the Notepad) — All your collected data is stored safely in a local database file. No cloud required, your data stays on your machine.
